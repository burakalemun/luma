## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an organic luxury ethos tailored for elevated lifestyle, wellness, and refined e-commerce experiences. It balances editorial intimacy with modern digital functionality, moving away from sterile corporate templates in favor of a tactile, inviting sanctuary.

### Personality & Emotional Response
The interface evokes calm discernment, tactile warmth, and understated prestige. Rather than shouting with stark artificial contrasts or hyperactive motion, it welcomes the user with the serenity of a sunlit architectural space filled with natural plaster, rich linen, and aged terracotta. Interactions should feel deliberate, effortless, and sensory.

### Design Movement & Influence
- **Warm Editorial Minimalism:** Spacious editorial grid structures, generous white space (represented through warm tones), and deliberate serif hierarchy drawn from prestige publications.
- **Organic Tactility:** Soft, warm-tinted layered surfaces, diffuse ambient drop shadows, and pillowy edge radiuses that create physical weight without decorative clutter.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in earth minerals, sun-baked clay, and organic botanicals. Contrast is achieved not through stark digital blacks and whites, but through deliberate, grounded organic tones.

### Color Roles & Application
- **Base Canvas (`#F9F5F0` - Cream):** The global canvas tone. Replaces standard white to eliminate screen glare and infuse an immediate sense of warmth and natural paper texture.
- **Secondary Surface (`#E8DDD0` - Warm Sand):** Used for cards, nested modules, structured sidebars, and input field backgrounds. Establishes tonal containment without hard dividing lines.
- **Primary Accent (`#C4714A` - Terracotta):** The lead interactive driver. Reserved for primary calls-to-action, key notifications, active pagination, and high-priority selections.
- **Secondary Accent (`#3D5A4C` - Deep Forest):** Imparts balance and botanical depth. Applied to secondary interactive surfaces, badges, deep contrast sections, dark card variants, and supporting metadata tags.
- **Brand Accent (`#8B2635` - Cherry):** A distinctive, concentrated accent tone. Deployed sparingly for signature brand highlights, exclusive tier ribbons, curated editorial features, or key delight moments.
- **Primary Text (`#2C2C2C` - Charcoal):** High-legibility text tone with softened contrast against Cream surfaces.
- **Secondary Text (`#7A7065` - Muted):** Secondary hierarchy for captions, timestamps, supporting labels, and disabled states.

## Typography

The typographic tension pairs the sculptural, high-fashion cadence of **Playfair Display** with the functional clarity and neutral precision of **Inter**. 

### Typographic Hierarchy & Editorial Rhythm
- **Playfair Display** is restricted strictly to headlines, hero statements, quote callouts, and collection titles. Never use it for running text, dense lists, or form labels.
- **Inter** governs all transactional moments, navigation links, microcopy, interactive forms, and body prose. Its neutral geometry ensures legibility against textured backgrounds.
- Capitalize small labels (`label-sm`) with subtle positive letter spacing (`0.05em`) to evoke quiet editorial captioning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy prioritizes generous breathing room and editorial composition over data density. 

### Grid & Composition Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid within a 1360px max container. Sections use generous outer margins (`3rem`) and broad column gutters (`1.5rem`) to create natural visual pauses.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` outer margins. Content blocks reflow from multi-column grids to paired clusters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer margins and compact `1rem` gutters. Stacks asymmetric desktop layouts into continuous, vertical reading streams.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Vertical component separation uses `space-lg` to `space-xl` to maintain a relaxed, unhurried posture. Avoid cramped groupings.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is crafted through warm tonal layering and soft ambient diffusions, steering clear of stark drop shadows or rigid black lines.

### Tonal Stratification
- **Ground Tier (Canvas):** Built on Cream (`#F9F5F0`).
- **Surface Tier (Cards & Blocks):** Built on Warm Sand (`#E8DDD0`) directly placed over the Cream ground. Depth is primarily established through hue difference rather than shadow.
- **Raised Tier (Floating Cards, Dropdowns, Modals):** White (`#FFFFFF`) or Warm Sand elevated with the signature ambient shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 24px rgba(44, 44, 44, 0.08);`
  - This low-opacity, wide-radius shadow renders elements as if floating softly above a linen surface under daylight.
- **Borders & Dividers:** High-contrast structural borders are prohibited. When structural definition is required, use a subtle tone blend (`rgba(44, 44, 44, 0.06)` or `rgba(122, 112, 101, 0.15)`).

## Shapes

The shape system embraces soft, natural curvature to reinforce organic luxury and touchability.

### Radius Scale
- **Small (`8px`):** Micro-elements such as inputs, badges, checkboxes, and inline tags.
- **Medium (`16px`):** Standard interactive surfaces including buttons, standard cards, and dropdown containers.
- **Large (`24px`):** Structural elements such as featured hero cards, modal dialogs, drawer panels, and editorial image frames.
- **Pill (`9999px`):** Filter pills, status indicators, and standalone icon utility buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled with Terracotta (`#C4714A`), text in Cream (`#F9F5F0`), with a 16px radius. Subtle hover shift to a deeper terracotta tone (`#B05F39`) accompanied by the signature ambient shadow.
- **Secondary:** Filled with Deep Forest (`#3D5A4C`), text in Cream (`#F9F5F0`), with a 16px radius.
- **Tertiary / Editorial:** Transparent background, Warm Sand border (`1px solid #E8DDD0`), Charcoal text. Hover fills subtly with Warm Sand.
- **Luma Touch (Signature Accent):** Cherry (`#8B2635`) fill with Cream text, reserved for exclusive promotional ribbons or limited-edition action flows.

### Cards & Panels
- **Standard Card:** Background Warm Sand (`#E8DDD0`), radius 16px or 24px, zero border. Padding driven by `space-lg`.
- **Floating / Elevated Card:** Background Pure White or Warm Sand, ambient shadow (`0 4px 24px rgba(44, 44, 44, 0.08)`), radius 24px.
- **Deep Contrast Variant:** Deep Forest (`#3D5A4C`) background with Cream (`#F9F5F0`) text for curated editorial quotes or category callouts.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Fully rounded (`pill-shaped`), 32px height, 12px horizontal padding.
- **Default State:** Warm Sand background, Charcoal text (`label-md`).
- **Selected State:** Deep Forest or Terracotta background with Cream text.

### Form Inputs
- Background Warm Sand (`#E8DDD0`) or pure White, radius 8px, height 48px.
- Border is borderless or faint muted tint (`rgba(44, 44, 44, 0.08)`).
- **Focus State:** 1.5px solid Terracotta (`#C4714A`) outline with no harsh glow, maintaining clean geometric calm. Placeholder text in Muted (`#7A7065`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size 20px, radius 4px (checkbox) or 50% (radio).
- Inactive state utilizes an Warm Sand fill with a muted outline.
- Active state fills with Deep Forest (`#3D5A4C`) featuring a Cream checkmark.

### Lists & Navigation
- List items feature generous vertical padding (`space-md`). Dividers, when present, use faint warm rules (`rgba(122, 112, 101, 0.15)`).
- Navigation links use Inter `label-md` with gentle terracotta underline indicators on hover and active states.
